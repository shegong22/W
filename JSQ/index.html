How it is calculated

stock concentration (mg/mL) = vial mass (mg) ÷ diluent volume (mL) aliquot volume (mL) = aliquot mass (mg) ÷ stock concentration (mg/mL)

1 mg/mL is the same as 1 µg/µL, so 0.200 mL and 200.0 µL are the same draw. Figures are reported to the precision each term carries — weigh and pipette to the resolution of your own instruments.

For laboratory research use only — not for human or veterinary use. This utility converts a labelled vial mass and a diluent volume into a stock concentration and the corresponding aliquot volume. It is not medical advice and not a guide for administration.
